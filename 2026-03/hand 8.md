# Hand 8

## Call

**Double**

## Hand

West, EW vulnerable, matchpoints.

`S AQT63  H K8  D K4  C AQ75`

Auction:

`West 1S - North 3H - East Pass - South Pass - West ?`

## Reasoning

I have far too much strength to sell out to 3H, but a unilateral 3NT is dangerous with only `Kx` in hearts. If North has a strong seven-card heart suit, notrump may lose control immediately.

Double keeps the auction flexible. It shows extra values and reopens for partner, who may pass with heart strength, bid a minor, or return to spades. At matchpoints this also preserves the chance to collect a penalty when partner was waiting with defensive values.
