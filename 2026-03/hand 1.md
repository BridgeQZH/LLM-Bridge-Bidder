# Hand 1

## Call

**3H**

## Hand

West, none vulnerable, IMPs.

`S T965  H K98753  D K72  C void`

Auction:

`North 1D - East 1S - South Pass - West ?`

## Reasoning

Partner has overcalled spades and I have four-card support plus a six-card side suit. In our competitive methods, a jump in a new suit below game is fit-showing. That makes 3H the most descriptive action: it shows heart length together with spade support, rather than pretending this is only a spade raise.

The hand is light in high cards, so I do not want to make a constructive 2NT raise. 4S is possible on shape, but 3H gives partner a much better picture: if partner has extra playing strength, spade game may be excellent; if not, we have competed while showing where our tricks may come from.
