# Hand 3

## Call

**3NT**

## Hand

West, both vulnerable, matchpoints.

`S A5  H KQJ5  D K4  C AK962`

Auction:

`North Pass - East Pass - South 1S - West Double - North Pass - East 2C - South 2S - West ?`

## Reasoning

The takeout double followed by further action shows a very strong hand. Partner's 2C may be weak, but it gives me an important source of tricks opposite my club honors.

At matchpoints, 3NT is the practical bid. I have a spade stopper, strong side-suit cards, and enough playing strength that game should be live opposite very little. Bidding more clubs understates the hand, while another double risks leaving partner to guess at too low a level.
