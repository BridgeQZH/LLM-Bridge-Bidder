# Hand 5

## Call

**3NT**

## Hand

West, NS vulnerable, matchpoints.

`S void  H AK5  D KJ3  C QJT7642`

Auction:

`South Pass - West 1C - North 3D - East Double - South Pass - West ?`

## Reasoning

Partner's double of the 3D preempt is takeout, showing the majors. I have only three hearts, but I also have a long club source of tricks and a diamond stopper.

At matchpoints, 3NT is the target contract when it is plausible. The spade void is not ideal, but partner's double strongly suggests spade length, and the opponents are unlikely to run spades from the North hand after a diamond preempt. 4H may play well, but 3NT uses my long clubs and protects the diamond holding.
