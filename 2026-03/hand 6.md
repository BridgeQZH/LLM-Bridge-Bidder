# Hand 6

## Call

**Pass**

## Hand

West, both vulnerable, IMPs.

`S 63  H 7  D KQJT963  C AQ6`

Auction:

`East 1H - South 2S - West 3D - North Pass - East 3NT - South Pass - West ?`

## Reasoning

Our 3D bid is game-forcing by system: after our major opening and an overcall, new suits at the three-level are game-forcing. Partner's 3NT should therefore be a real choice of strain, usually based on spade values and a hand suitable for notrump.

I have an excellent diamond suit, but I do not have enough outside controls to insist on slam or remove from what may be the best game. If partner can stop spades and use my diamonds, 3NT rates to score well. Bidding again risks turning a likely plus into a strained five-level contract.
