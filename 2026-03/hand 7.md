# Hand 7

## Call

**4D**

## Hand

West, both vulnerable, IMPs.

`S Q5  H A6  D AQ7642  C KQ6`

Auction:

`North 1S - East 2C - South 3S - West ?`

`3S` is preemptive.

## Reasoning

Partner has made a natural club overcall, and South's preemptive raise has stolen the 3NT space from us. Although I have the spade queen, `Qx` is not a robust stopper when North has opened spades.

4D is my best descriptive action. In our methods, new suits after partner's overcall are forcing for one round, so this shows a strong diamond suit and values without guessing notrump. Partner can raise diamonds, bid clubs, bid notrump with a real spade control, or continue toward slam with extras.
