# Hand 2

## Call

**4H**

## Hand

West, both vulnerable, IMPs.

`S KQ  H AKT5  D Q62  C AK64`

Auction:

`North 3D - East Pass - South Pass - West Double - North Pass - East 4D - South Pass - West ?`

## Reasoning

My takeout double showed a strong hand with interest in the unbid suits. Partner's 4D cue-bid is not trying to play diamonds; it is a forcing action asking me to describe my hand further.

With four hearts, only two spades, and a balanced powerhouse, 4H is the natural next step. I do not want to leap to notrump or slam without knowing whether partner's strength is major-oriented. If partner has spades or extra values, they can continue; if partner has hearts, we have found our likely strain.
