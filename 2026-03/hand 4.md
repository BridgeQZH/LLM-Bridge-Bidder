# Hand 4

## Call

**3C**

## Hand

West, EW vulnerable, IMPs.

`S void  H 85  D JT952  C 976542`

Auction:

`North 1S - East Double - South 2S - West ?`

## Reasoning

Partner's double is takeout, and my hand has extreme minor-suit shape with no spades. Passing would be too pessimistic: we have an eleven-card minor-suit holding and very little defense to spades.

Because we are vulnerable and my high-card strength is almost nonexistent, I choose the cheapest practical action, 3C. It keeps us in the auction and lets partner correct to diamonds with a better fit. Jumping to 4C would put too much pressure on partner opposite a hand that may produce very few high-card tricks.
