# Hand 6

## Call

**3NT**

## Hand

West, both vulnerable, IMPs.

`S AKJ73  H 9  D K32  C AK96`

Auction:

`West 1S - North Pass - East 2H - South Pass - West 3C - North Pass - East 3H - South Pass - West ?`

## Reasoning

The 2H response is game-forcing in our 2/1 structure. After I show clubs, partner's 3H rebid strongly suggests a real heart suit, often six cards. My singleton heart means I should not raise hearts without a much clearer reason.

3NT describes the hand best: strong side-suit honors, a diamond guard, no heart fit, and no need to invent a spade rebid. Partner can still remove 3NT with a very distributional hand, but opposite many game-forcing heart hands this is the most natural contract.
