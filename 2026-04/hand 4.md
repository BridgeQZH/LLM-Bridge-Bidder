`S 7  H AKQ743  D Q106  C A82`

`East 1D - South Pass - West 1H - North 1S - East 2D - South 3S - West ?`

## Reasoning

Partner has shown diamonds twice and the opponents have competed hard in spades. My hand has a strong six-card heart suit, a singleton spade, and enough high-card strength to expect game to have play opposite many minimum diamond rebids.

At matchpoints, 4H is the practical call. It gives the major-suit game a chance instead of settling for a minor partscore or guessing to defend 3S. Double would be awkward because my spade holding is not defensive, and 4D understates the playing strength of the hand.
