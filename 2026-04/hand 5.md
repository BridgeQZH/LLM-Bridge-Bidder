# Hand 5

## Call

**4C**

## Hand

West, EW vulnerable, IMPs.

`S T5  H K6  D 63  C AKQT753`

Auction:

`East 1H - South 3S - West ?`

## Reasoning

After our major opening and an overcall, new suits are forcing for one round, and new suits at the three-level are game-forcing. Here 3C is no longer available, so 4C is the cheapest natural club bid rather than a voluntary fit-showing jump.

The hand is far too strong and too trick-oriented to pass, but it is not a heart raise with only doubleton king. 4C shows the source of tricks and lets opener choose between 4H, 5C, 3NT if somehow appropriate later, or further action with extras.
