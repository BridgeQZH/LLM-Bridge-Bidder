# Hand 1

## Call

**5D**

## Hand

West, EW vulnerable, IMPs.

`S 5  H T  D AKQ9653  C KJ95`

Auction:

`North 3H - East 4S - South Pass - West ?`

## Reasoning

Partner's vulnerable 4S overcall over a 3H preempt should be a serious playing hand, but my hand is not a normal spade raise: I have only one spade and a powerful independent diamond suit. Passing 4S may still work, but it hides the main feature of the hand and may leave partner in a poor strain if spades are not solid.

5D is descriptive. It says that I have real values and a long diamond suit, while leaving partner room to choose 5S with a self-sufficient spade suit or to continue if the diamond suit solves partner's losers. I am not using 4NT as RKCB because the singleton spade makes key-card information much less useful than strain information.
