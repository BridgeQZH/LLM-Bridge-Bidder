# Hand 2

## Call

**Double**

## Hand

West, both vulnerable, IMPs.

`S Q8  H K93  D KJ52  C Q763`

Auction:

`North 1S - East Pass - South Pass - West ?`

## Reasoning

In the balancing seat I have about an opening bid, only two spades, and tolerance for all unbid suits. Passing would let the opponents buy 1S too cheaply.

Double is the flexible system action: it is takeout-oriented, keeps hearts, diamonds, and clubs in play, and lets partner choose the strain. A natural 1NT would be less attractive with only a partial spade stopper and no source of tricks.
