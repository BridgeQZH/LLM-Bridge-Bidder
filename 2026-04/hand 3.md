# Hand 3

## Call

**4S**

## Hand

West, none vulnerable, IMPs.

`S AJ  H 5  D AQ1086  C AJ1086`

Auction:

`West 1D - North 3H - East 3S - South 4H - West ?`

## Reasoning

By our competitive methods, a new suit by responder at the three-level after our minor opening and an overcall is game-forcing. Partner's 3S therefore shows a real spade suit and values, not merely a competitive noise bid.

The hand is excellent for spades: two strong trumps, a heart singleton, and prime minor-suit controls. Still, I only have two-card spade support and partner was forced to bid at the three-level under pressure, so I do not want to drive past game unilaterally. 4S accepts partner's strain and shows where I want to play unless partner has enough extra shape or values to move again.
