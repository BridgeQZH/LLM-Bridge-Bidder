# Hand 7

## Call

**4NT**

## Hand

West, none vulnerable, matchpoints.

`S KQT863  H 53  D AQT5  C A`

Auction:

`East 1NT - South Pass - West 2H - North Pass - East 2S - South Pass - West 3D - North Pass - East 3NT - South Pass - West ?`

## Reasoning

Over 1NT, 2H transfers to spades. The 3D continuation is a natural game-forcing second suit, showing slam interest or at least a hand too strong to merely place the contract. Opener's 3NT denies a clear spade fit and suggests notrump as the strain.

With 16 HCP, a six-card spade suit, a strong diamond suit, and a singleton club ace, I am too strong to pass 3NT. Since spades have not been agreed, 4NT is quantitative rather than RKCB. It invites slam while letting opener pass with a minimum 15-count or accept with suitable extras.
